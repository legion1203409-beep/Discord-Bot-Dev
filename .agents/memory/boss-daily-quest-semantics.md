---
name: Boss daily quest semantics
description: How to reconcile the rare boss daily quest's participation wording with its defeat requirement.
---

Treat participation in the epic/legendary daily boss quest as contributing positive damage to a boss that is defeated. Count all contributing fighters, not just the final hitter, including fighters who fell during the encounter. Merely attacking a boss that later escapes does not complete this quest.

**Why:** The requirement combines “ucide un boss” with “Participă la un boss epic/legendar”. Counting the victorious group satisfies both without making daily rewards depend on who gets the last hit.

**How to apply:** Keep victory-based quest settlement separate from attack activity and escape rewards. The promised daily heart is additional to ordinary boss loot; do not replace that loot or remove pre-existing daily quests.
